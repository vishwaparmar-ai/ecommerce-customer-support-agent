# Refund Policy

## Eligibility

A refund can only be issued once the following conditions are all met:

1. The associated return has reached **Completed** status (the returned item has been
   received back and accepted by the warehouse).
2. The original order's payment was actually collected — i.e. the payment status is
   **Paid**. Orders with a Pending or Failed payment are not eligible, since no money
   was ever collected to refund.
3. No refund has already been issued for that return. Only one refund per return is
   permitted.

## Refund Amount

Refunds are issued for the full amount of the original payment associated with the
order. Partial refunds are not currently supported — a return covers the entire order,
and the refund matches the full payment amount.

## Refund Method

Refunds are credited back to the original payment method used at checkout (card, UPI,
netbanking, wallet, or — where applicable — adjusted against future orders for cash on
delivery purchases). There is no option to redirect a refund to a different payment
method.

## Refund Timeline

Once a refund is created and processed, it is marked as either:

- **Completed** — the refund succeeded and the original payment is marked Refunded.
- **Failed** — the refund could not be processed (e.g. a payment gateway error); in
  this case, support staff must investigate and manually retry.

Refunds typically complete within 5–7 business days of the return reaching Completed
status, depending on the customer's bank or payment provider.

## What Cannot Be Refunded

- Orders where the return was Rejected (see Return Policy) are not eligible for refund.
- Orders where payment status is anything other than Paid (Pending, Failed, or already
  Refunded) cannot be refunded again.
- Shipping charges, if any were collected separately, are non-refundable unless the
  return is due to a ShopFlow error (wrong item shipped, damaged in transit).
