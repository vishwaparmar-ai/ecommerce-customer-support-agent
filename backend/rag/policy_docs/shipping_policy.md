# Shipping Policy

## Order Fulfillment Stages

Once a payment is successfully collected, an order moves through the following
shipment stages:

1. **Preparing** — the order is being packed at the warehouse.
2. **Shipped** — the package has left the warehouse and is with the carrier.
3. **In Transit** — the package is moving through the carrier's network.
4. **Out for Delivery** — the package is with the local delivery agent for final delivery.
5. **Delivered** — the package has reached the customer.

## Carriers

ShopFlow ships using a panel of courier partners, including BlueDart, Delhivery, Ekart,
DTDC, and India Post, depending on the delivery location and package size. A tracking
number is assigned once the shipment is created.

## Estimated Delivery

Every shipment is assigned an estimated delivery date at the time of dispatch, typically
3–7 days from the order date depending on the destination. Customers can track the
shipment's live status and estimated delivery date via their order details.

## Delayed Shipments

A shipment is marked **Delayed** when it has not reached the customer by its estimated
delivery date. Delays can occur due to logistics network congestion, weather, regional
disruptions, or address issues. See the Delivery Exceptions document for how delayed
and lost shipments are handled.

## Lost Shipments

In rare cases, a shipment may be marked **Lost** if the carrier is unable to locate or
deliver the package after an extended period. Customers with a lost shipment should
contact support — this typically results in a replacement order or a full refund,
handled as a support case rather than a standard return.

## Shipping Address

The shipping address is captured at the time of order placement and cannot be changed
once the order has moved past the Preparing stage. Address changes on orders still in
Preparing should be raised as a support ticket.
